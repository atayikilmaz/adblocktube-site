Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

AdBlockTube is a web browser for video, music and social websites. We built it to collect as little as possible. We have no user accounts, no servers that store your data, and we never sell personal data.

What stays on your iPhone

Your settings, app order, queue, history of what you played and the websites' own data (cookies, sign-ins, site storage) are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Websites you open

When you open a website in AdBlockTube, your device connects to that website directly, just as Safari would. That website's own privacy policy applies to what you do there, including signing in. AdBlockTube blocks many of the ads and trackers those websites load.

Services the app talks to

  • Filter updates. The app downloads its ad-blocking filters from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). GitHub sees your IP address, as with any download. Nothing about you is sent.
  • Dislike counts and sponsor segments. If these features are on, the app asks Return YouTube Dislike and SponsorBlock about the video you are watching (SponsorBlock receives only the first characters of a hashed video ID). They see your IP address.
  • Purchases (Adapty). If you buy or restore Pro, purchases are processed by Apple and verified through Adapty, which receives an anonymous app profile ID, device and app version information and your purchase history so the app knows you have Pro.
  • Ads in the free version (AppLovin). The free version may show one small banner ad. The ad provider AppLovin and its partners receive device information, your IP address and how you interact with ads, and, only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt, your device's advertising identifier. Where the law requires consent, you are asked first. Pro has no ads. See AppLovin's privacy policy.
  • Siri. If you allow Siri, requests like “play … on AdBlockTube” are processed by Apple.

Tracking

AdBlockTube itself does not track you. Advertising partners may track only if you allow it in the App Tracking Transparency prompt; you can change this anytime in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Children

AdBlockTube is rated 17+ because it can open any website, and it is not directed at children.

Your choices

You can turn features off in the app's Settings, deny tracking, use Pro to remove ads, and delete all data by deleting the app.
