AdBlockTube / Privacy

Privacy policy

AdBlockTube is a browser. What you watch, search and sign in to stays between your iPhone and the sites you open.

Effective 11 November 2026

AdBlockTube is made by Shidin (“we”). This policy covers the AdBlockTube iPhone app and this website, which uses no cookies or analytics.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your browsing, search and watch history
  • Cookies and sign-ins for the sites you open, kept in the app’s own web storage
  • Your Up Next queue, saved queues, settings and the blocked-ads count

We do not run servers that receive this data, and we never sell personal data.

Your choices

  • When the app first asks, choose whether ads may be personalised and whether apps may track you. Change it later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Turn off SponsorBlock or dislike counts in the app’s Settings.
  • Delete the app to remove everything it stored on the device.
  • Pro removes the ad banner and its ad requests.

Children

AdBlockTube is rated 17+ because it can open any website. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

We will post changes on this page with a new effective date. Questions go to shidin@shidin.com.
