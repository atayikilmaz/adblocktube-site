Support

Need help, found a site that doesn't work, or have an idea? Write to ata.yikilmaz@gmail.com. In the app, Settings › Apps › a site › Report a Problem fills in the details for you.

Common questions

A site stopped working or shows ads again.

Sites change often. Fixes are sent to the app over the air within hours, without an App Store update. Pull down to reload; if it stays broken, report it.

How do I watch at 2×?

Press and hold a short or regular video. Slide down while holding to lock 2×. Tap the badge to go back to normal speed.

Playback stops when I lock my phone.

Make sure the video was playing before you locked. Use the Lock Screen controls to resume.

Restore Pro on a new device.

Settings › Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Cancel a subscription.

Settings app › your name › Subscriptions › AdBlockTube Pro.
