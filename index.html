How ad blocking works on iPhone

  • Spatial Audio on AirPods
  • Sleep timer on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island

Background play on iPhone, step by step

Hold for 2×, slide down to lock it

Press and hold any Short or video to watch at double speed. Slide down while you hold and 2× stays on, even as you swipe to the next Short. Shorts get their own native feed that loads the next ones ahead.

YouTube links open here

Tap a YouTube link in WhatsApp, Messages or Mail and it plays in AdBlockTube, not in Safari. Turn on the Safari extension once and change it anytime in Settings.

Your own Up Next queue Pro

Play Next or Play Last on any video, save a queue under a name and play it again later. In the car, CarPlay shows your music on the dashboard and “Hey Siri, play…” works.

Locked with Face ID

Privacy Lock opens the app only with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode, and the app switcher shows nothing. Music and videos keep playing while it is locked.

No address bar. Each site is an app

Sites open with native tabs, search and a real video player, built for each one. Search from Home or from a site’s own search tab.

Questions

Is AdBlockTube free?

Yes. Ad blocking, background play, Picture in Picture and every other feature except three are free. Pro removes the app’s own banner and adds CarPlay and the Up Next queue.

How do I play YouTube in the background on iPhone?

Open a video in AdBlockTube and lock the screen or switch apps. The sound keeps going and the Lock Screen shows play, pause and skip. The background play guide has the details.

Does it download videos?

No. AdBlockTube streams from the sites you open and never saves videos to your iPhone.

Can I sign in to my YouTube account?

Yes. Sign in on the site inside the app as you would in Safari, and your subscriptions, playlists and history are there. Signing in is optional.

What happens when a site changes and an ad gets through?

The filters and site scripts update over the air, without an App Store update. The app checks for a new set when you open it and in the background.

Does it work on iPad or Android?

It is made for iPhone and needs iOS 26 or later. An iPad layout and Android are not available yet.

Is AdBlockTube made by YouTube or Google?

No. AdBlockTube is an independent browser and is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by any of the sites it opens.
