How to use YouTube Picture in Picture on iPhone

Play a video in AdBlockTube and swipe up to go home. The video keeps playing in a small window you can move, resize or tuck to the side.

Updated 11 November 2026 · By Shidin, makers of AdBlockTube

Steps

  1. Install AdBlockTube and open a YouTube video.
  2. Start playback, then swipe up from the bottom edge or press the Picture in Picture button in the player.
  3. Drag the window to any corner. Pinch to resize it, or swipe it off the edge to keep only the sound.
  4. Tap the window and choose the expand button to return to the app.

Picture in Picture needs to be on in iOS Settings → General → Picture in Picture → Start PiP Automatically.

Hide the video, keep the sound

Swipe the floating window off the side of the screen. The video hides behind a small tab and the audio continues, just like background play with the screen locked.

Send it to a TV

Open Control Center, tap the AirPlay icon in the now-playing card and pick an Apple TV or AirPlay TV. Playback controls stay on your iPhone.

Shorts and 2× speed

Shorts get their own native feed. Hold any Short or video to watch at 2×, and slide down while you hold to lock that speed for the next ones.
